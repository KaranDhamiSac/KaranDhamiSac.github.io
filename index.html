Recurve Archery for Beginners

Archery is a sport that rewards patience, consistency, and good form more than raw strength. Most beginners start with a takedown recurve bow because the limbs come off the riser, so you can start with a light draw weight and upgrade the limbs as you get stronger. Before buying gear, it helps to learn the main parts of the bow and how they work together. You can read more about the history and design of this bow on the Wikipedia page for recurve bows.
